One local Python application. Notes come from the VEVOR piano (MIDI input), the mouse or the computer keyboard; they sound through a connected MIDI output or a fallback (OS synthesizer or a built-in tone generator). Recordings and the songbook are JSON files in `records/`; user choices (skin, effects, equalizer animation, octave doubling) live in a per-user `settings.json`.
