description: Desktop MIDI controller and practice app for a VEVOR digital piano
---

synth-mido connects a computer to a VEVOR 88-key digital piano over MIDI, and also works without it. Players can play on an on-screen piano or the computer keyboard, record and play back takes, listen to a public-domain songbook, and watch Synthwave-style visuals, all in one Tkinter desktop window with 17 selectable skins.
